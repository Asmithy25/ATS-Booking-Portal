---
name: Visual direction
description: Shared visual language for the Ayden public site and portal surfaces.
---

The current product direction is autumn editorial care: warm cream, terracotta, ochre, muted olive, and deep cocoa accents; an expressive serif for emotional display moments; a clear sans-serif for utility; and mono labels for operational context. The public site should feel welcoming and story-led, staff should feel focused and operational, and clients should feel personal and unhurried while still sharing the same underlying system.

**Why:** The previous surfaces felt like separate warm utility screens. A shared visual vocabulary makes the product feel intentional and memorable while the role-specific hierarchy keeps each experience fit for its user.

**How to apply:** Preserve this relationship when adding pages or states. Change hierarchy and interaction density by role before changing the core palette, typography, geometry, or motion language.
